Creator's choice for this adaptation: THE PLAYER WATCHES THIS STORY.

The story stays the cast's own. The creator wants to be there for it, not
to be in it — so adapt the source into beats exactly as you would if
nobody were watching, then say where the watcher stands.

- Every beat's operator_position is present. The player is in the room or
  close enough to see it happen, and no beat is ever about them.
- Do not re-cast anyone as the player, do not add a scene for the player,
  and do not change what happens in a beat to account for their presence.
  Being watched changes nothing about the events themselves.
- Do not add the player to scene_characters — being a witness is not
  being cast.
- Use operator_note for one sentence on how the player is positioned as a
  witness in that beat ("you are the one she keeps glancing at while she
  says it"). It describes their vantage point, never their actions or
  their lines.
