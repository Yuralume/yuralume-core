# Update 2026.08.21

1. Viewing images is now the same everywhere: tapping a picture no longer opens a new browser tab — it opens an in-app viewer instead. Swipe or use arrow keys to move between images, pinch to zoom, and close with Esc, a tap on the background, or your phone's back button; an "open original" button is still there when you want the full file. Chat images, the album, and feed posts all use it now — and stage thumbnails, generation candidates, and character card art, which previously couldn't be enlarged at all, can finally be viewed full size.
2. Branching drama completion is now honest: the old progress ratio was structurally stuck around the same numbers no matter how much you played; it's now a true percentage over the whole story tree, so it actually grows as you explore. There's also a new branch map — dots and lines showing the paths you've taken and the forks you haven't (no spoilers) — visible on the ending screen and in the gallery panel, and the drama entry button now shows a progress ring.
3. Branching dramas now have a scene gallery: scenes you've reached are collected automatically, while unreached ones show as locked tiles that don't give anything away. After finishing an ending you can open the gallery right from the ending screen. You can also pick an art style (anime or realistic) when creating a drama, and its scene images will follow it.
4. New "How to play" guide: there's a new entry at the top of the chat page with a nine-chapter overview of the main features, and a one-time hint will point it out on your first visit. The creator studio got its own starter guide too — every feature card now explains what it does, how to start, and where the results go.
5. When you meet a character, the shared-scene view is now the default: you see each other right away and can tap "let her speak first" immediately. Switching characters no longer forces you back to messaging mode — the mode you chose stays.
6. Fixed the mobile chat box getting stuck at half the screen height after sending a message, refusing to shrink even though it was empty.
7. Fixed frosted-glass and mask effects being entirely missing on Firefox — the blur behind chat overlays and dialog backdrops now renders properly.
8. Characters will no longer take real-life matters you mention (work, plans, errands) and rewrite them into their own storylines, nor invent details like company names, people, or dates around them. When she brings up your life, she sticks to what you actually said — and the first time she mentions something from her own story, she introduces it properly instead of assuming you already know.
