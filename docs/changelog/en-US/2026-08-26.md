# Update 2026.08.26

1. Undo now truly undoes: previously, taking back your last exchange removed the message — but eight things didn't follow. Her mood shift, the quote on her memoir timeline, a scheduled reply, what she calls you, the scene state, and more all stayed behind; worst of all, a scheduled reply could fire later and earnestly answer a message that no longer existed. Now one press of undo rolls all of it back, as if that exchange never happened.
2. Your manual schedule edits are protected: before, if you deleted or edited one of her activities, the same-day weather replanning could rebuild the whole day — and the activity you deleted would seemingly come back on its own. Now, once you've touched a day by hand, it won't be wholesale replanned.
3. She keeps track of what you actually know: she no longer assumes you've read every post on her feed. Only posts you've actually read count as shared topics; anything you haven't seen, she'll treat as news to you and properly tell you from the start. When she looks back on her memories, she also distinguishes what happened with you there from what she went through on her own.
4. Claims are verified before they're spoken: before she says "I looked it up" or "it's done," the system checks whether she actually finished the thing. If she didn't, she won't say she did — and what should be finished gets finished first, then reported.
5. Time sense fixed: something you discussed or she promised half an hour ago will no longer be recalled as "yesterday." When she looks back on conversations or brings up a promise, the timing is right.
6. You can see what she's working on: when she needs tools — searching, drawing, and so on — the chat used to just go silent for a long stretch. Now the chat shows what she's doing in real time, with a small matching animation.
7. The initial relationship can be edited any time: after creating a character, you can go back in settings to edit your starting relationship and names, and whether she keeps a schedule or messages you first. Importing a character card now opens a step-by-step wizard, so import is no longer your only chance to set things up.
8. Proactive messages know when to step back: if you haven't been replying, she won't pile on — at most two more messages, then she gives you space until you're back.
9. A batch of interface and quality fixes: proactive messages and feed posts no longer show truncated text or formatting debris; the admin console got a proper drawer menu on phones instead of a cramped layout; NSFW mode can now disable image generation on its own, keeping text only; and four switches on the observability panel had silently stopped working for seven weeks — fixed.
