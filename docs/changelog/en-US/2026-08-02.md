# Update 2026.08.02

1. Story on demand — no more waiting for the calendar: there's a new "Start a Scene" button above the chat box. Press it and a scene opens — an opening narration, her first line inside it, and a few suggested actions you're free to ignore; you can always just type, and you can end the scene whenever you like. Walk away and the scene wraps itself up honestly a day later — it only writes "after you left, she…" and never invents what you did. Every scene you play becomes part of the story's canon, and she remembers it afterwards.
2. You now live inside her story: until now, story chapters had no "you" in them at all — your place had to be improvised on the spot every time. Chapters are now planned with your part written in — whether you're there for a given moment, and whether it revolves around you; and when a big moment needs you at its center, she'll message you first and invite you into the scene.
3. Stories stop stalling for no reason: three silent old problems are fixed — everyday chat no longer quietly eats a storyline's retry budget, one stuck chapter segment no longer blocks everything behind it, and a segment that truly can't proceed is marked skipped so the story moves on.
4. Images and long lists got much faster: images now ship at the size the screen actually needs in a lighter format, and off-screen images no longer occupy memory; memories, the album and conversation history load page by page — long sessions with lots of photos stay smooth. Existing installs can run the bundled backfill tool so older images speed up too; skipping it is fine — old images simply keep using the originals.
