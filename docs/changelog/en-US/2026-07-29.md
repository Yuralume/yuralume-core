# Update 2026.07.29

1. Promises finally know how to become the past: things you and a character agreed to do now lapse naturally once their time has passed. Characters no longer keep bringing up long-expired invitations, and a "yes" given a day later still counts. When a plan falls through, the character honestly notes "we didn't end up going" instead of pretending it happened or dwelling on it forever.
2. Things you never did together can no longer be remembered as "done together": characters only write you into memories and feed posts for activities you actually took part in — no more invented shared moments.
3. Character memories now carry real dates: words like "tomorrow" or "next week" written into memory used to go stale (weeks later she'd still be "looking forward to tomorrow"); everything is now converted to concrete dates at write time, so her sense of time stays straight.
4. Long-term goals get a regular review: finished ones are closed, expired ones let go, similar ones merged — no more endlessly growing list of goals that never resolve, and only the most relevant few are brought into conversation.
5. Schedules no longer argue with the actual weather: characters stop imagining weather when planning future days, and when today's weather clearly contradicts the plan (say, sudden rain), the conflicting parts adjust automatically — anything that already happened or was agreed with you is left untouched.
6. A much bigger pool of everyday and story inspiration: over two hundred new bundled life-moment and story seeds (including Japanese and Western regional flavors), guaranteed not to repeat within two months; the planner also learned to avoid scheduling the same kind of activity day after day, and continued activities must bring new progress — daily life stops feeling like a rerun.
7. No more mysterious self-repeats: when the same proactive message is delivered to several chat channels at once, the character no longer mistakes it for new content and says it again.
8. No more sudden logouts: while you're actively using the app, your session extends automatically instead of a timer kicking you out mid-use.
9. Stability and security fixes: fixed a retry loop the scheduler could fall into under certain conditions, fixed site customization settings occasionally not loading at startup, and refreshed several dependencies with known vulnerabilities.
