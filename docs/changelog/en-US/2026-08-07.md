# Update 2026.08.07

1. Video posts are generated in the background now. Posting a video used to mean waiting out the whole pipeline with everything else on hold; generation is queued in the background instead, so you can keep chatting and the video shows up on the feed when it's ready. The first frame also comes from a still image that is actually drawn, so the opening second is really her rather than a guessed-at picture.
2. Deleting or resetting a character now clears everything that belonged to it. Previously some of the scattered data — memories, album, feed posts, schedule — could survive the delete as orphans; the delete now walks every place character data is used and clears them together.
