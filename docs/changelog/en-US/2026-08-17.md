# Update 2026.08.17

1. Branching drama now knows where *you* stand: when starting a drama you choose your place in the story — the lead (the story revolves around you and the narration speaks to you directly), a supporting character on the scene, or a pure observer — and you can add a line about who you play (e.g. "her long-lost brother"). The narration and plot are written around that position. Reaching the end of a route is no longer a dead end either: replay the drama down a different branch, adapt the route into a full script and keep developing it, or export the whole transcript as a keepsake.
2. Drama scene images can now be redrawn: fill in one that failed to generate, or swap out one you don't like — only the image changes, the story stays put. Progress display during a drama is also more accurate now.
3. New "Your persona": you can now tell each character who you are — your identity, your background, even the secret settings only the two of you share. From then on she remembers it whenever she speaks to you: everyday chat, messages she sends on her own, keeping an appointment, story openings, comment replies. Edit it any time from the top of the chat page; the old "my current status" field in personal settings retires in its favor.
4. In Stage mode you can now let her speak first: a new button on the input bar for when you don't know how to start, or just want to hand her the moment. Optionally add context for the scene (e.g. "I walk through the door"), or leave it empty.
5. The web app no longer gets stuck on an old version: previously, a tab you kept open would keep using the old interface after an update until you manually cleared site data. It now detects new versions and updates itself automatically.
