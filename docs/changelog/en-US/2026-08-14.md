# Update 2026.08.14

1. Scene openings feel like a story now. They used to be a few short lines of stage directions, sometimes dropping names nobody had explained — leaving you unsure where you were or what was going on. An opening is now several paragraphs of novel-style narration, with imagery, movement and voices; any name the story introduces gets explained the moment it first appears, and the scene closes in on you before she says her first line.
2. The quick-reply suggestions after an opening speak as *you* again. They would occasionally offer *her* lines instead of something you would say, so tapping one meant speaking on her behalf. They are now always in your own first person. Also fixed: on phones (notched screens, or when the address bar pops in), the story-picker dialog no longer clips its buttons.
3. When she promises you something, she actually does it. If a character says "I'll look that up later" or "I'll send you a photo when I'm back", she now really goes and looks it up, or gets the photo ready, and comes back with the result — instead of being stuck at "still working on it" forever. And if it doesn't work out (nothing found, the photo won't come out), she tells you honestly and follows up later, rather than making something up.
4. Security update: refreshed the low-level components used for deployment images and encryption.
