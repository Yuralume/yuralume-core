# Update 2026.08.05

1. Seven new official characters (ten in total): each comes with a full personality, their own daily schedule, and a distinct art style. Browse the official gallery and bring home the ones you like.
2. The official gallery now syncs with the official catalog: new characters and card fixes appear right away, no version update needed. Prefer not to connect? Turn it off and the gallery simply shows your own cards only — cards you import always live locally and are never affected.
3. Official cards now ship in three languages: switching languages used to translate the card on the spot, spending your model budget and making you wait. Chinese, English and Japanese are now built in, so cards simply appear in your interface language — no translate button, none of your tokens.
4. Side-story covers and memoir backgrounds now load as size-optimized images: faster pages, less bandwidth.
