# Update 2026.08.11

1. Full character backup: pack up a character you've raised — persona, memories, conversations, schedule, photo album and all — into a single encrypted backup file protected by a one-time password. Keep it as an offline copy, or restore it on another install to bring them home again; even a brand-new environment with no characters yet can restore straight from the file. Keep the password safe yourself: without it, nobody (us included) can open the file.
2. You can now follow up on news a character shared: previously, once she brought a news story up, asking about it later drew a complete blank. Now the stories she mentioned in the last few days stay at hand along with the original source, so follow-up questions actually get answers.
3. Scrolling back through older messages now shows a floating "jump to latest" arrow that takes you straight back to the newest message — and we fixed the chat sometimes stopping partway down when the page first loads or you switch characters.
4. When a messaging-platform connection has bad credentials (say, a mistyped bot token), the account card now shows what's wrong and how to fix it, and the connection recovers on its own once you do — instead of retrying every few seconds in the background.
